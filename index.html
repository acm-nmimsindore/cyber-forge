1. Event Overview & Structure

A TWO-DAY PROGRESSION MODEL

Day 1 focuses on learning and direct interaction with the invited cybersecurity expert, while Day 2 empowers participants to apply the concepts through practical tasks and demonstrate their solutions.

DAY 1 — EXPLORE & LEARN

LEARN THE THREAT

Provides deep expert insights into real-world cybersecurity, understanding adversary tradecraft, threat identification, and defensive practices through direct industry interaction.

  • Inauguration & Welcome: Chapter opening and expert introduction.
  • Expert Interaction Session: Industry insights, real-world experiences, and discussion.
  • Core Cybersecurity Workshop: Threat analysis, security architectures, and defense.
  • Live Cybersecurity Demonstration: Expert-led demonstration connecting workshop concepts with practical scenarios.
  • Interactive Q&A: Technical questions, career discussions, and industry interaction.
DAY 2 — BUILD & DEFEND

BUILD THE DEFENSE

Application of Day 1 learning through practical problem-solving, guided mentoring, participant-led project demonstrations, expert feedback, and formal recognition.

  • Hands-on Activity Briefing: Task objectives, guidelines, and expected outputs.
  • Practical Cybersecurity Challenge: Hands-on application, analysis, and problem-solving.
  • Guided Sessions & Mentorship: On-site technical support and guidance as students refine work.
  • Participant Demonstrations: Students showcase and defend their completed solutions.
  • Expert Feedback & Certificates: Professional critique, key takeaways, and certificate distribution.
2. Core Objectives

EVENT OBJECTIVES

Defined to provide an engaging bridge between academic theoretical concepts and real-world cybersecurity practices.

01

Foundations & Real Challenges

Introduce students to fundamental cybersecurity concepts and real-world security challenges faced across modern networks.

02

Expert-Led Exposure

Provide practical exposure through expert-led demonstrations and guided activities illustrating attack and defense scenarios.

03

Problem Analysis & Presentation

Encourage students to critically analyze cybersecurity problems and articulate structured, practical defense strategies.

04

Defensive Thinking Mindset

Develop persistent awareness of secure coding practices, vulnerability discovery, threat identification, and defensive thinking.

05

Direct Industry Interaction

Enable direct interaction between students and an external cybersecurity professional, fostering career insights and mentorship.

06

ACM Technical Platform

Promote ACM as an active platform for hands-on, industry-oriented technical learning and student community engagement.
